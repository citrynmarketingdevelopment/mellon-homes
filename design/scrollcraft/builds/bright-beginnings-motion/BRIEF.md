# Mellon: Bright Beginnings, in motion

This is a design study based on the user's existing brief and selected concept.
Motion choreography not quoted below is an authored proposal, not a user quotation.
Deliverables: an additional editable concept in mellon.pen, motion storyboards,
a mobile layout, and an isolated HTML motion demonstration. Do not edit the live site.

## Brief evidence and creative decisions

1. **Vibe.** User: "I like the first one." Preserve its playful pink, cream,
   berry, Archivo Black and Nunito Sans language. Proposed evolution: generous
   photographic compositions with one memorable reveal.
2. **Journey.** User: "i dont want to change the LIVE hhero on the page."
   Earlier: "MELLON's listings personally will be more front page than a widget
   showing other listings for sale." Preserve the complete live hero, then
   Mellon property showcase, specialist support, development, Ian, broader
   search, and direct contact.
3. **Energy.** User selected: "One dramatic property reveal, then calmer
   specialist sections (recommended)."
4. **Feeling and remembered moment.** Proposed: recognition, anticipation,
   delight, reassurance, possibility, confidence, readiness. Remember the pink
   panels opening onto a home, not an endless animation sequence.
5. **Signature.** Proposed: "New keys. New possibilities." on two pink panels
   that part at different depths to reveal the actual supplied exterior photo.
   A stable caption carries the identical-build disclosure throughout.
6. **Aesthetic range.** First concept is the approved reference: playful and
   accessible, not minimal black-and-white or a cinematic dark world.
7. **Structure.** Distinct sections, preserving the existing hero. Only one
   extended pinned moment. No worldflight, autoplay, scrub video or scroll hijack.
8. **Assets.** Use the original mascot and supplied flyer photography. Keep
   original image colors. No generated listings, invented Ian portrait, or
   testimonials. Original flyers remain untouched.

Audience: Bakersfield and Shafter buyers, sellers, and people facing complex
property situations. Desired belief: Ian can help me understand a practical next step.
Primary contact label: "Let's talk", matching the live header.

## Grammar and fingerprint

**Brokerage walkthrough**, a constrained adaptation for the preserved live hero:
the existing navigation is untouched; one property encounter follows it; sensitive
services are visitor-selected in normal flow; projects are grouped by stage; the
page ends with direct human contact. Bans: changing the hero, continuous worlds,
multiple pinned chapters, autoplay, fake feed data, attention-demanding motion
inside sensitive copy.

The eight stock grammars were considered. Filmic and continuous-world replace
the protected hero; editorial and typographic poster suppress the existing
character; live surface implies a product demo; gallery alone hides specialist
care; split stage forces a comparison that does not exist; cutlist is too hurried.

Registry was empty. No prior scroll-craft fingerprint rows to compare.
Previous static concepts are retained, not overwritten.

### Feel-check difference

The intended curve is recognition, anticipation, delight, reassurance,
possibility, confidence, readiness. The visual review shows one distinct reveal,
then settled reading and visitor-selected content. This is a design assessment,
not a measured user-emotion result.

The first screenshot pass exposed hidden process steps in Ian's section.
The stagger needed both the entrance marker and a numeric delay. Correcting
that restored the confidence beat and removed an unintended blank area.
A new browser check now verifies that all three steps reach full opacity.
The resolved property view has a short hold before it releases into the
interior photographs; no further pinned sequences were added.
